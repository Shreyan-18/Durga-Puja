I was finding so many problems while planning my pandal hopping this year.

Juggling queue times across different hours, figuring out which pandals are close together, avoiding traffic traps, and finding good food along the route was such a headache.

So instead of endlessly roaming the internet and scrolling through random lists, I decided to build a website to solve it. 🚀

Introducing: Durga Puja Pandal Hopping Optimizer 🪔

---

💡 Why generic map apps don't work for Pujo:
Standard GPS tools treat routing like any normal day. But during Durga Puja:
• A pandal like Suruchi Sangha or Sreebhumi has a 90-minute queue at 9:00 PM, but drops to 20 minutes by 3:30 AM.
• Crossing between South and North Kolkata during peak evening hours is a recipe for getting stuck in gridlock.
• Real hopping needs planned stops for food, tea, and adda without making massive detours or blowing your budget.

🛠️ What I built into the platform:

🏛️ 164+ Curated Bengal Pandals: Covering South Kolkata, Behala, North, Central, Salt Lake, VIP Road, and Howrah with crowd tiers and darshan windows.
🍽️ Route-Aware Dining: Automatically matches 46 iconic Kolkata eateries (Arsalan, Peter Cat, Mitra Cafe, Golbari, Balwant Singh Dhaba, etc.) along your path with minimal detour.
🍱 Up to 6 Scheduled Meals: Plan everything from evening rolls and dinner to midnight chai and early morning breakfast.
💰 Budget & Cost Limits: Set a maximum per-person budget (₹200 – ₹4,000) and the optimizer picks transit modes and dining options that fit within your cap.
🚆 Suburban Rail & Metro Gateways: Guides commuters through All-Night EMU local trains (Sealdah South line), Kolkata Metro, and pedestrian barricades.
🗺️ Interactive Visual Route: Door-to-door path line visualization powered by PyDeck and Streamlit.
